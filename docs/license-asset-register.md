## 1. Legal Rules & Disposition Taxonomy

The root `LICENSE-MIT` and `LICENSE-APACHE` files cover eligible source code only. They do **not** grant automatic rights to fonts, icons, brand marks, sample project files, or test corpus binaries. Every asset evaluated carries one of three formal dispositions:

- **`approved`**: Permissive open-source license (OFL-1.1, ISC, MIT/Apache) with verified sidecar attribution.
- **`restricted`**: Proprietary storytold brand asset or trademark (e.g. `LICENSE-brand.txt`); must **not** be used in Clover Motion production builds.
- **`hold/unknown`**: Third-party sample projects (.psd, .aep, .prproj, .fig) or model binaries where upstream commercial rights are unconfirmed.
